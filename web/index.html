How it works

A public address in, feelings out

She never touches your wallet. A relay reads the public chain and tells her what your address did; she does the rest.

01 · the chain

Your fills happen

pump.fun curves, PumpSwap, Raydium, LaunchLab, Meteora — every venue the firehose carries, every quote token.

→
02 · the relay

Matched to your address

Seeds your cost basis from history, dedupes by signature, converts stock- and USDC-quoted trades to SOL-equivalent, forwards price ticks for what you hold.

→
03 · Yui

Reacts, keeps score

Buy, profit, loss, big win, rug — each one a pose, a line, a sound. The board marks your bag to market on every tick.

Honest PnL

Realised against average cost, so partial exits are right. Unrealised from the token's own market cap, so stock-paired coins don't produce nonsense.

Under a second

The relay listens to the live firehose; a fill reaches her about as fast as it reaches the chart.

Public by design

Anyone can connect, under caps — a few sockets per address, a few wallets per session. Or run the relay yourself; it's in the download.

The name

唯 — sole, only, unique

It seemed like the right word. She isn't a service with a million users wearing the same face: she runs on your machine, watches the one address you hand her, learns your name, and keeps score of your day.

No account. No cloud. Nobody else's girl — dress her how you like, and the version you end up with is one nobody else has.

One trader, one companion. That's the whole idea, and it's what the name says.

Take her home

The same character, the same code, either way you run her. On your desktop she stands over every window; in Chrome she stands on the terminal you trade from.

🖥️ On your PC

The desktop app

A folder you unzip and run — no installer, nothing in the registry. She lives on your taskbar over every window, stays up while you trade, keeps her own settings, and works whatever you have open.

  1. Download the zip and unpack it anywhere.
  2. Run Yui.exe — she says hi and asks your name.
  3. Paste the address you trade from into her Wallet tab.

Open source

Every line of her is public

Electron, Three.js and three-vrm. The desktop app, this website and the relay server live in one repository under the MIT licence.

TradingCompanion/trading-companion

Read her, fork her, build her

The renderer that runs on this page is the app's renderer, bundled unchanged. Her tour, her physics, her wounds, her voice — it's all in src/renderer.js. The relay is server/pet-relay.js. Nothing private ships: the packager refuses to build a release that contains an address, a token or a wallet.
